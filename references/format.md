# The format, decoded

Breakdown of the viral "game cutscene character sells a t-shirt" reels, from a frame-by-frame and
audio analysis of two examples (24 s and 30 s).

## Structure

1. **Hook line (0-2 s)**: a question or observation that is true and slightly absurd.
   "did you know there's a country where chewing gum is illegal?" / "it's funny how every government
   wants you to use public transport".
2. **Explain (2-6 s)**: one sentence of fact.
3. **The joke (6-12 s)**: "like, imagine being the guy who..." Delivered in close-up.
4. **The twist (12-20 s)**: pivots to a claim that sets up the product. In the references the
   t-shirt slogan *is* the punchline, spoken while the character points at it.
5. **End card (last 3-4 s)**: hard cut to a real studio photo of the product on a plain background,
   URL as a caption, voice says "get yours at <url>, link in bio".

## Picture

- **Look**: early-2000s Japanese action-game pre-rendered cutscene. Slightly angular character model,
  painted textures, chunky sculpted hair, simple skin shader, strong coloured rim light.
- **Character**: one recurring character with a loud silhouette: distinctive hair, something on the
  head (goggles), gloves, statement trousers. Always wearing the product.
- **Location**: a real, recognisable place tied to the fact. Lighting uses two colours that match the
  product (purple and orange in one reel, white and orange in the other).
- **Shots**: medium with a hand gesture, wide full-body, tight close-up, medium pointing at the
  product, product still. 4 animated shots and 1 still. A cut every 4-6 seconds on a sentence break.
- **Camera**: static or a slight handheld drift. One tracking walk-and-talk in the second reel.
- **Frame**: 4:3.

## Sound

- **Voice**: one speaker, early 20s, General American, dry and a little smug, about 150 words a
  minute, close and dry with no room reverb. A small laugh or exhale after the last line.
- **Music**: a quiet lo-fi / ambient bed around 80-90 BPM, 12-15 dB under the voice, continuous.
- **SFX**: none, or a very low room tone.

## Captions

White, bold, geometric sans, lowercase, soft black shadow, centred in the lower third, 2-7 words per
card, changing with the speech. Quoted speech inside the rant is in italics with quote marks. The
end-card URL uses the same style.

## Why it holds attention

- The first frame is a strange character in a familiar look, which reads as "what is this".
- The first line is a claim you want to check.
- Every 4-6 seconds the framing changes.
- The product is on screen for the whole video without the video being about the product until the
  last line.
